02 / How I work

A four-step path from messy process to automation that holds

  1. 01

    Business diagnosis

    Map the process, find where time and revenue leak, agree on what success looks like before writing anything.

  2. 02

    Automation design

    Choose the smallest architecture that holds: data flow, triggers, fallbacks, and who owns each step.

  3. 03

    Technical implementation

    Build it: APIs, workflows, tracking, integrations, instrumented so failures are visible early.

  4. 04

    Stakeholder management

    Keep clients, engineers and commercial teams aligned on scope, trade-offs and timelines.
